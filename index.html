LumaTube Privacy Policy

Search, browsing and downloads

Search queries, URLs and network requests are sent to the selected websites and content providers, which receive your IP address and connection information and apply their own policies. The embedded browser stores site cookies and login sessions locally. LumaTube does not require its own user account or send your passwords to the developer.

Local files

Settings, download-task history and file metadata are stored on your device. Downloaded media is stored in the app library and may be copied to Movies/LumaTube or Music/LumaTube according to your settings. The app does not automatically upload your media library to a developer server. Sharing or exporting sends the selected file to your chosen app, destination or folder.

Advertising

Google AdMob provides ads and Google UMP manages consent choices where applicable. Google advertising services may collect and share IP address (which may estimate approximate location), app/ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, advertising ID and device/app-set identifiers for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google advertising data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Ad personalization depends on consent, region and service configuration. Manage the advertising ID through Android settings and use the app's ad privacy option when available. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Permissions and deletion

Internet access retrieves content and ads. Notification permission displays download status. Foreground services and wake locks support ongoing transfers. Older Android versions may request storage write permission; newer versions save app-created media through system APIs without all-files access. Delete media through the library or clear app data in Android settings. Shared gallery/export copies may remain after uninstall until you delete them. Clearing app data also clears local embedded-browser data. Third-party data retention/deletion follows providers' policies. Contact us for assistance. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure.

Children and updates

LumaTube is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children; contact us about any concern. Updates to this policy will carry a revised date on this page.
